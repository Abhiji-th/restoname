Acme Corp Employee Handbook

1. Remote Work Policy:
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days a week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory in-office days for team meetings. 

2. IT Security:
Do not write passwords on sticky notes. All laptops must be locked when stepping away from your desk. Use a VPN when connecting to public Wi-Fi.

3. Expense Reports:
Meals are covered up to $50 per day during business travel. Alcohol is not a reimbursable expense. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days via the Concur portal.
